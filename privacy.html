Privacy

What we collect, and what we do not

Last updated 11 September 2026. This describes what this site actually does — it was written by reading the site's own code, not from a template.

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies. None. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no ad network, no session recording, no fingerprinting.
  • We collect what you type into a form, and a count of which pages are viewed. That is the whole list.

If you fill in a form

Our forms ask for some of: your name, email address, company or website, the offer you are interested in, and a message. You choose what to type. Submissions are sent to our own server (a Cloudflare Worker at leads.steelworksintelligence.com) and stored in our customer records.

We use it to reply to you, to deliver what you asked for, and to send follow-up about our services. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers. Ask us to delete it and we will.

If you just read

A small script records that a page was viewed: the event name, the page path and title, and — if you click a call-to-action or a product link — the link you clicked. It sets no cookie and does not identify you. Counts, not people.

Services we rely on, and what they see

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub receives your IP address in its server logs, as any web host does.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, so Google receives your IP address. This is the only third party that sees you as a result of loading a page.
  • Cloudflare runs the workers that receive form submissions and page counts.

How long we keep things

Form submissions are kept while they are useful for the enquiry or the customer relationship, and deleted on request. Page counts are aggregate and are not tied to a person.

Your choices

Email admin@steelworksintelligence.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to stop hearing from us. We will act on it. If you are in a place with specific data rights — the UK or EU under GDPR, or California under the CCPA — this is the address to use, and we will honour those requests.

Children

This site is for business use and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
