What makes these different from a prompt dump: each one ships with the prompt itself, when to reach for it, and a tip on the part people get wrong.

A real one, in full:

Email subject lines — "Write 10 subject lines for an email announcing [PRODUCT/NEWS] to {audience}. Under 50 characters each. Mix: 3 curiosity, 3 benefit-led, 2 urgency, 2 plain-direct. No clickbait, no ALL-CAPS." When: every send; the subject decides the open. Tip: test the plain-direct ones — they win more often than you'd think.

Notice the specificity — a named mix, a character limit, an explicit ban. That is the difference between a prompt that returns ten usable lines and one that returns mush.
