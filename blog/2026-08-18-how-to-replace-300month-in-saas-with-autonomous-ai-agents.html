Step 3: Add the Guardrails

The OpenClaw platform, utilizing its 22 autonomous AI agents (dubbed SteelWorks Intelligence), incorporates several checks to prevent potentially problematic output from being delivered. Primarily, before any outreach email is sent, the system verifies the lead’s data against a curated list of known spam traps and domains associated with fraudulent activity. Furthermore, each email is scanned for overtly promotional language, excessive capitalization, or any elements flagged as potentially violating OpenClaw’s established content guidelines. This automated scanning processes prioritize avoiding communications that could be perceived as misleading or disruptive.

Beyond individual email checks, SteelWorks Intelligence employs a "reputation scoring" system for both the author (SteelWorks Intelligence Systems, Acworth GA) and the leads themselves. This score is continually updated based on engagement metrics – such as email open rates and click-through rates – as well as internal reports of negative feedback or complaints. Leads receiving a low reputation score are automatically demoted, meaning their data is less likely to be used for outreach.

Finally, the system maintains a detailed audit trail of all actions taken by the AI agents, allowing for immediate identification and correction of any issues. This traceability provides a layer of accountability and enables rapid adjustments to the system's behavior to maintain the highest standards of communication and data integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an autonomous AI agent fleet?

A set of scheduled AI agents that run business tasks — publishing, research, monitoring, outreach — without a human starting each one. They execute on a schedule, write their results to disk, and escalate only when something needs a decision.

Can this run without paid API credits?

Yes. The stack here runs local models through Ollama for generation, with hosted providers used only where a local model genuinely cannot do the job. The recurring software cost is zero.

How do you stop AI agents from inventing results?

Every claim has to trace to a data file, and publishing is gated: a post with no measurable backing, or metrics that do not reconcile against the ledger, is held rather than published.

What hardware does it need?

A single machine is enough to start. This fleet runs on a MacBook with local models, a scheduler, and a browser automation bridge.

How long before automation produces results?

Operational results are immediate — jobs run unattended from day one. Audience and revenue results follow normal content timelines, which are measured in months, not days.
