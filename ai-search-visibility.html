What is checked

Five legs. Each one says when it could not be assessed.

1. Technical readiness

Can the AI crawlers get in (robots rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google‑Extended, and the firewall in front of them)? Is there an llms.txt summary, a sitemap, structured data that says what and where you are, a clear title, description and headline, a page that loads light?

method

weight

2. What the engines already know

We ask assistants about your business by name and classify the answer: recognised, partial, or unknown. If they have you confused with someone else, that shows up here.

method

weight

3. Who gets cited instead of you

Five questions your customers actually ask, put to the assistants. We record who they name. We never invent a competitor citation to make a point.

method

weight

4. What the rest of the web says about you

Assistants recommend a local business on outside evidence, not on its own site: a complete Google Business Profile, reviews they can read, the same name, address and phone everywhere, listings on the directories they cross‑check, and something dated this quarter. We look for each one and show what we found — and what we could not.

method
homepage + schema + 2 web searches + map lookup
weight
20%
