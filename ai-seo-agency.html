What Changed

Buyers are asking answer engines before they ever reach your website.

Traditional SEO still matters, but it is no longer enough to have a site that only targets ranked blue links. Businesses now need clear entity signals, direct answers, source-backed proof, FAQ coverage, schema, local trust, and a website that is easy for both people and AI systems to understand.
